# Customer Support Team Lead — Harbourline Retail (Remote, Spanish/English)

We are a growing e-commerce retailer with customers across Latin America and the US. We are hiring a Customer Support Team Lead to run a bilingual support team.

## Responsibilities
- Lead a team of 10–15 support agents and manage schedules and coverage.
- Handle escalated complaints and improve resolution times.
- Train new hires and maintain the support playbook.
- Report weekly KPIs (response time, CSAT, unresolved cases).

## Requirements
- 4+ years in customer service, 2+ years leading a team.
- Experience with a **helpdesk tool** (Zendesk, Freshdesk or similar).
- Advanced spreadsheet skills.
- **Professional English** (written and spoken).
- Strong coaching and communication skills.

## Nice to have
- Experience in e-commerce.
- Customer service or leadership certifications.
