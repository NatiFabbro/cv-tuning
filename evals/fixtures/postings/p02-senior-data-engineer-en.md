# Senior Data Engineer — Brightfield Commerce

**Location:** Remote (Americas timezones) · **Type:** Full-time

Brightfield Commerce runs a marketplace platform used by thousands of independent sellers. Our data platform team is looking for a senior engineer to own the reliability and cost of our pipelines.

## What you'll do
- Design, build and operate batch pipelines on AWS.
- Lead technical decisions and mentor a small team of engineers.
- Improve observability, data quality and cost efficiency of the platform.

## Requirements
- 7+ years of experience in data engineering.
- Strong **Python** and **SQL**.
- Production experience with **Apache Spark** and **Airflow**.
- Experience with **AWS** (S3, Redshift or similar) and infrastructure as code (**Terraform**).
- Experience leading or mentoring engineers.
- Fluent English.

## Nice to have
- **dbt** for analytics engineering.
- **Kafka** or other streaming technologies.
- AWS data certifications.
